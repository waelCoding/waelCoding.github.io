01 — About

About Me

I am a Computer Science & Information Systems graduate from the Faculty of Science and Engineering, and a Software Engineer currently working at Ytech in the Software Department, where I develop and maintain business software solutions and troubleshoot ERP systems.

My journey at Ytech began with a 6-month internship, during which I studied business processes on the Workcube ERP platform and learned its web-based architecture. I was then assigned to build a Point of Sale (POS) system using C# .NET, which I successfully delivered — and was subsequently hired as a full-time Software Engineer.

Since then, I have worked on RFID-based warehouse and asset management systems, developed applications, built reports, resolved ERP issues, and created APIs to integrate our systems with external parties or databases via Linked Servers. I also build my own personal projects using Node.js, SQL Server, Flutter, SQLite, PHP, Python and MySQL.

I hold internationally recognized certifications from Microsoft in Azure, Python and Data Analysis, as well as US-accredited certifications in Programming and a 3-month Accounting certificate. I am also TOEFL certified in English, enabling me to work effectively in international environments.

I care about writing software that is practical, well-structured, and built to last — whether it's an enterprise ERP workflow, a REST API, or an offline-first mobile application.
